## Ürün açıklaması (uzun)

```
Tonelle (https://tonelleapp.com) is a personal colour analysis service for beauty, sold by Doribleg Trade Ltd (UK).

What the customer gets: the user uploads one selfie and answers a short quiz. Our AI analyses skin undertone, contrast and features and classifies the user into one of 12 colour seasons. The result is a personal report: a colour palette, foundation undertone, recommended lipstick, blush and eyeshadow shades, colours to avoid, and a skin analysis (skin type and concerns with care suggestions). This is analysis, classification and recommendation, comparable to an in-person colour consultation.

Makeup preview (secondary feature): the user can see a few predefined makeup looks (e.g. "natural", "evening") applied to their own selfie. There is no free-text prompt and no way to generate arbitrary images, art, text, music or video. No images of other people, celebrities or adult content; inputs are limited to the user's own selfie and fixed presets. Outputs are private, not published and not stored on our servers.
```
